# ShopSmart Policy Documents
Version: 2026.1 | Effective: January 2026

## Return Policy
Section: General Returns

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Section: Electronics Returns

Electronics including laptops, phones, and tablets may be returned within 15 days of delivery.
Opened electronics (laptops, desktops) can be returned within 7 days if there is no physical damage and all original accessories are included.
Software products and digital downloads are non-refundable once activated.

Section: Books Returns

Books may be returned within 30 days if unopened. Opened books may be exchanged for store credit only.

## Refund Policy
Section: Refund Timeline

Once the returned item is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
The refund is credited to the original payment method.
For bank transfers, please allow an additional 3-5 business days for the amount to reflect.

Section: Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted for items returned after the standard return window (within 45 days) at the discretion of the customer support team.

## Shipping Policy
Section: Standard Delivery

Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. Express delivery takes 2-3 business days.
Free standard shipping on orders above $50.

Section: Prime Membership Shipping

Prime members receive free standard and express shipping on all orders above $25.
Same-day delivery is available in selected metro cities for Prime members.

## Warranty Policy
Section: Laptop Warranty

All laptops come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects.
Physical damage, liquid damage, and software issues are not covered under warranty.
Extended warranty (2 years total) is available at the time of purchase for an additional fee.

Section: Phone Warranty

Smartphones carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Screen damage due to drops is not covered.

## Membership Benefits
Section: Prime Membership

Prime membership costs $99/year and includes:
- Free express shipping on all orders above $25
- Early access to sales and deals
- 2% cashback on all purchases
- Free returns for 30 days

Section: Premium Membership

Premium membership costs $199/year and includes all Prime benefits plus:
- Dedicated customer support line
- 5% cashback on all purchases
- Free same-day delivery in eligible areas
- Extended return window of 45 days
